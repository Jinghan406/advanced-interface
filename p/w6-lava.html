Lava

Black and white, on real photographs. A drop of wax seeps into the item under the pointer and stretches from row to row. The pointer is also a heat source: the letters near it soften, sag and drip, then cool slowly once it moves on. Pressing gathers the wax into a bead and melts the whole word.

States

Enabled
Black surface · white label
Hover
Wax seeps in from where the pointer crossed the edge and fills the item (radius 10). Text inverts.
Between items
Leading edge moves fast, trailing edge lags, so the wax stretches across the gap
Pressed
Wax gathers into a bead around the pointer, Ø about 0.8 × item height
Release
Bead splashes back out to fill the item, then the menu closes
Submenu open
Parent item stays filled. A drop bulges through the right edge toward the submenu
Photo under wax
Difference blend turns the thumbnail into its negative
Text · hover
Letters within 56 of the pointer melt by distance: blur up to 0.15 em, stroke up to 0.085 em, sag 0.14 em, stretch 30%, drips up to 0.75 em
Text · leave
Letters cool back over about 1 s, slower than they heated
Text · pressed
Whole word melts to at least 75%
